3. What is sent to our service

When you're online, the app connects to the Vastu Prop AI service (hosted on Supabase, our cloud provider):

  • An anonymous account. The app signs in without asking who you are. This creates a random ID so we can apply daily usage limits. It contains no name, phone number or email.
  • Your Vastu result, to write "In simple words". Room names and types, which direction each room is in, its status (Ideal, Okay, Avoid), the score, and the suggested fixes. Never your photo, plan image or location.
  • Questions you ask Disha. The text of your question, the recent messages in that chat, and the room positions and directions from your result, so Disha can answer about your home.
  • Daily usage counts. How many explanations and questions your anonymous ID used today, to enforce the free daily limit.
  • Anonymous usage counts. So we can see which features help people, the app notes when you: open the app; open the compass (and whether your phone has one); set the direction with the sun; finish a floor plan check (the score, how many rooms were scored, which way the plan faces, the main door's direction, and which room types sit in a direction to avoid, such as "kitchen in the South-West"); finish a room check (the room type, which way it faces, and how many fixes and good points it got); look up a home number (only the single digit 1 to 9, never your flat number); download a PDF report; see the "In simple words" explanation (and whether it was written on the phone or by our service); ask Disha a question (only the kind of answer, such as a "what if" answer, never your question or the answer text); and save a check. Each note also carries a random ID made on your phone, the app version and the kind of device (Android app, iPhone or web). It never includes your photos, plans, location, flat number or anything you type. You can switch this off at any time in the app under About & privacy ("Share anonymous usage to improve Vastu Prop"); when it's off, nothing is sent and any unsent notes are deleted from your phone.

Our service records basic technical logs (which feature was used, how long it took, whether it worked) to keep it running. These logs don't contain your questions, photos or location.

To write explanations and answers, our service may use an AI language model, run by us or by an AI provider we contract. That model only receives the result and question described above. The AI explains your result; it never changes your score.

5. Permissions

Motion and compass: to find direction. Location: optional, for compass correction and the sun check. Camera: only for the room check, when you tap it. You can refuse any permission; the app offers another way (for example, typing the direction).

  • Disha questions and results sent for explanations: processed to answer you and not kept as a record of your chats.
